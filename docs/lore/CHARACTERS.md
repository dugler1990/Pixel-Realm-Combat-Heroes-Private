# Characters: playable heroes and their openings

Lore draft, 2026-11-14. Bullets meant to be turned into story text later. World facts used: the world map
(`levels/world_map_reference.png`), `notesonthestorylore` (12,000 BC, before known history, Anunnaki feel,
born in a cave and surviving the ice, ayahuasca-style trips for the big changes), factions Eskimo and Jungle Tribe.

## Shared thread
- **The Falling Night.** A light crosses the sky and strikes beyond the Ice Wall.
- Every hero's opening happens that same night. The barb waking naked in the snow is one of them.
- Nobody explains it yet. The Anunnaki feel: the ones from the sky, old tech, the Wall itself.
- **Evolution** = a vision (the ayahuasca-trip note) at set levels (e.g. 10, 25). The path is picked inside the
  vision; the model changes.
- Target: 5-20 playable. Core heroes get a first-person opening; unlockables (bosses, allies, monsters) don't need one.

## Core heroes

### Barb (Frostreach). Exists
- Wakes naked in the snow. No memory. Kept mysterious.

### The ice wizard (the Ice Wall). Idea only, not being built (2026-11-14)
Low level: early twenties, lean and underfed, shaved head, faint frost-blue ritual lines (crown, nape, arms,
collarbones), clean-shaven, pale wind-burned skin, barefoot, a short wool wrap at the hips.

Three takes on his cult (pick or mix):

**A. The Rime Choir** (preferred: explains the naked start)
- Cult in caves cut into the inner face of the Ice Wall, near Glacier's Bight.
- Belief: the Wall was not made by winter. It was *sung* shut by the ones from the sky.
- Magic = a voice frozen into ice. Elders carry shards that still hum with the dead's songs.
- Novices are never told what is behind the Wall. Only elders "listen at the seam".
- Rite: at 21 a novice is put out of the Wall gate naked, alone. Survive the night, come back an initiate.
- Opening: "They close the gate behind me. The cold is the test, they said. Then the sky splits, a light falls
  past the Wall, and the gate never opens again."
- Low level because he is unfinished: initiated by no one, carrying half a song.
- Hook: the barb was found naked in the snow too. An older novice who failed?
- Fits the existing level `levels/Frostreach/ice_wall_gate`.

**B. The Unmelted**
- They freeze their own alive, waiting for the sky ones to return and wake them.
- He is a sleeper, frozen at 22; the falling light cracks his ice.
- Opening: "I wake inside the Wall. My breath is the first warm thing in here. Every other sleeper's face is dark."
- He doesn't know if he slept one year or a thousand. His spells are half-remembered rites.

**C. The Keepers of the Seam**
- A guard order at a crack in the Wall. Something on the other side keeps knocking.
- Teaching: cold is not death but *stillness*. You can hold anything still, even a heart.
- He is the youngest keeper. When the seam opens, he is the only one who runs.
- Opening: "Everyone stayed. I ran. I don't know what came through."
- Driven by guilt. Others call him a coward or a traitor.

Paths:
- **Keeper (good)**: shields, slows, mends allies. Clear ice crystals grow from his shoulders and spine, his skin
  turns blue-white, a halo of frost.
- **Hoarfrost (necromancer)**: freezes the dead into ice-thralls. Black frostbite climbs his limbs, his eyes frost
  over, he goes gaunt, bone shows through the ice.
- **Choir (pure caster, optional)**: his voice is the weapon. Runes glow down his throat and chest, his ribs turn
  half to ice.
- Play (fits the armour notes): light body, blasts throw him far, immune to cold, a bonus on slippery ice.

### The Ashborn (Ashen Hellscar), demon spawn
- Opening: "I claw out of cooling rock. Something huge above me is still screaming. My hands are wrong. They
  are small."
- Low level: imp-sized, grey ash skin, glowing cracks, horn stubs, no wings.
- Paths: **Tyrant** (huge, horns, hide like plate, brute fire) / **Smoke** (thin, smoke wings, blinks) /
  **Hollow** (the fire goes out, turns to obsidian glass, turns on the Hellscar).
- As player 1: a runaway. Demons hunt him, people fear him. The Frostreach cold hurts him.

### The Apprentice (Eldenridge), human wizard
- Opening: "My master is dead on the floor. I lost an hour. The book is open. My hand is black to the wrist."
- Low level: plain robe, satchel, a stick for a staff.
- Paths: **Lightward** (white and gold, rune halo, shields and healing) / **Necromancer** (the black spreads up the
  body each level: gaunt, bone, raises the dead).
- Overlaps the ice wizard's good/necro split; keep one or make them differ (fire/light vs ice).

### Kesh (south jungle: Singing Marshes / Verdant Vale)
- Opening: "I fall out of the canopy. The village is empty. The prints in the mud are bigger than a hut."
- Low level: small and wiry, body paint, loincloth, blowpipe.
- Paths: **Beastbound** (claws, crouched, animal traits) / **Spirit-walker** (shaman; the vision trips are his
  people's) / **Venom-hand** (poison master; the slow-death venom).
- Play: fast, climbs, no armour, darts.

### The Ice Champion (Shiverhay / Winterguard), Eskimo
- Opening: "The ice took my boat. I walk home across Frostmere. Under the ice, something follows me."
- Low level: fur parka, bone goggles, harpoon.
- Paths: **Whale-caller** (huge, blubber, harpoon pulls enemies in) / **Ice-speaker** (frost shaman).
- Play: immune to cold, slides on ice.

### The Buried One (Sunspine Dunes)
- Opening: "Sand in my mouth. My own clan buried me at Dunewatch. I don't know why. I dig up."
- Paths: **Sand-sorcerer** / **Scorpion-blade** (assassin).

### The Deep Miner (Stonebreak March, Deep Peak)
- Opening: "The mine fell. Thirty days in the dark. I come out half-blind with a lantern, and the stone hums."
- Paths: **Stone-fused** (rock skin, slow tank) / **Blaster** (bombs; the blast damage type).

### The Drowned (Windward Isles, Seafarer's Rest)
- Opening: "I wake on the beach with my lungs full of sea. I should be dead."
- Paths: **Tidecaller** / **Wreck-dead** (rots, slowly turning undead).

## Unlockables (no opening)
- Hellscar warlord: the boss the Ashborn ran from.
- Jungle elder shaman: ally, Kesh's teacher.
- Eskimo elder: ally, holds the RTS camp.
- The thing under Frostmere: play as the monster.
- A Wall-beast (giant or mammoth type) from the Ice Wall.
- The Starborn: late. Tall, too perfect, carries old tech. Tied to the barb's mystery. Hold back.
