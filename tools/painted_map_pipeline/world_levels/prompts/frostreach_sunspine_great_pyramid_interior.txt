4. WHAT A PYRAMID IS (this is the whole job)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A pyramid is mostly SOLID STONE. Looking down with the roof off, you see the
INNER FACES of the stepped tiers — concentric diamond terraces of massive
sandstone blocks, getting smaller toward the centre, like a stepped well.
That stepped funnel IS the pyramid. It must fill the entire outer diamond.

Cut into that mass, and only that:

  - A processional walkway from the lower-right doorway at (2580, 2280), about
    400 px wide (the collision door gap), running inward toward the centre.
    Sand drifted in at the threshold. Heavy stone jambs. The corridor MUST
    meet the outer diamond at that doorway — misalignment with the exterior
    door is a fail.
  - Two small side chambers opening off that walkway, one on each side.
  - A large burial chamber at the inner end of the walkway: slab floor,
    a stone sarcophagus, inner wall faces with reliefs.
  - The rest of the diamond is stepped masonry, not more rooms. Stairs down
    to a deeper dungeon come later; do not invent them yet.

Large parts of the image should be "not a room" — just the stepped interior
of the pyramid. That is correct.
