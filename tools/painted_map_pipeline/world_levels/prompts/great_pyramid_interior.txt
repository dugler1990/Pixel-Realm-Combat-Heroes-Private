THE ONE ENTRANCE
There is a single doorway, already painted, on the LOWER-RIGHT face of the pyramid. It is
held out of the mask - do not paint over it, do not move it, do not redraw it.
The pyramid has EXACTLY ONE entrance. Do not draw another doorway, arch, stair or opening
anywhere in the outer ring. If you are about to draw a second entrance, do not.

BUILD IT IN THIS ORDER. THE ORDER MATTERS.

STEP 1 - THE FLOOR, AS ONE SINGLE CONNECTED SHAPE
Before any walls, decide the walkable floor. Picture it as ONE piece of paper laid flat
on the ground, all in one piece, nothing detached:

    behind the door  ->  entrance corridor  ->  long gallery  ->  burial chamber

                                             \-> side room     (joined to the gallery)

    there for at least 600 px. The door must open onto floor, never onto stone.
  - Every part touches every other part. You can walk from the door to any room without
    crossing stone.
  - If any piece of floor is not joined to the rest, the picture is wrong.

STEP 2 - WALLS AROUND THE EDGE OF THAT FLOOR
Now lay stone walls along the outline of that floor shape, about 120 px thick, the same
thickness everywhere.

  - Where a room meets the corridor, the wall STOPS: leave a clean gap 250 px wide with a
    jamb block each side. The floor runs through that gap unbroken - you can see straight

  - Everywhere else the wall is continuous and closed.
  - A room drawn as a complete unbroken rectangle of wall is WRONG. Every room has at
    least one gap.
  - Walls are stone courses seen from above, with a visible top face and a clear edge on
    both sides. Never a change of colour on flat ground.

STEP 3 - ALL REMAINING SPACE IS SOLID
Everything in the mask that is not floor or wall is the solid mass of the pyramid: plain
unbroken courses of massive blocks. Most of the picture is this. That is correct.

SCALE - READ THIS BEFORE COMPOSING
A character is 150 px. The whole space is about 21 x 18 characters - one small crypt, the
